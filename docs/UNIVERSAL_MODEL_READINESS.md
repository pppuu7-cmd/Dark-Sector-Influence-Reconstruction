# DSIR universal-model readiness strategy

**Date:** 2026-08-25

## Strategic rule

DSIR must not prematurely collapse into a universal dark-sector model. The primary research program remains model-agnostic comparative reconstruction: embed qualitatively different dark-sector and modified-gravity families into common response channels, compare their geometry, identify nulls/degeneracies/sign reversals/channel migration/nonseparability, and record new hard or negative findings.

In parallel, estimate the minimum number of independent latent response coordinates/functions required by the validated atlas. This minimum dimension is a research output, not an assumption. A raw catalog SVD, a convenient three-channel summary, or the current discriminator hitting set is not evidence for a three-parameter dark sector.

## Why this matters

The eventual universal model should be informed by the mature DSIR atlas rather than imposed on it. It must be able to query the atlas for:

- which response blocks are genuinely independent after observational/gauge/precision validation;
- which families lie in exact or approximate null spaces of particular operators;
- which apparent degeneracies migrate or reverse between geometry, temporal growth, density/velocity, slip/lensing and small-scale transfer;
- whether scale and time can be separated at all, or whether an irreducible `k x z` interaction must be retained;
- how many latent coordinates are stable to family prior, within-family sampling, covariance, solver precision and channel removal;
- which observables fail as low-dimensional compressions for scale-dependent models;
- what withheld family/model can be reserved for a genuine out-of-sample prediction.

## Readiness criteria for beginning a universal model

Begin the dedicated universal-model construction only when all of the following are substantially satisfied:

1. **Comparative atlas maturity:** the main frozen families have validated mappings across the response blocks physically relevant for their mechanisms; missing blocks are distinguished from validated zeros.
2. **Cross-family semantic bridges:** gauge, total-matter/tracer and solver conventions are validated well enough that density, velocity, metric and transfer comparisons are meaningful.
3. **Observational layer maturity:** geometry, growth/RSD, shape/window and slip/lensing mappings are sufficiently validated that theory-space geometry is not mistaken for survey distinguishability.
4. **Latent-dimension stability:** a candidate minimal number of independent coordinates/functions survives prior, sampling, covariance, precision, domain-support and channel-removal stress tests. No fixed number, including three or four, is assumed beforehand.
5. **Nonseparability audit:** candidate latent coordinates have been checked for hidden interaction structure such as the scale-time term found in Experiment 045A; a low-dimensional model may not replace a joint response surface by independent one-dimensional summaries unless this is validated.
6. **Withheld validation plan:** at least one physically distinct family/model or response regime remains hidden from model construction and can test genuine prediction rather than interpolation.
7. **Claim discipline:** G7/G8 boundaries remain respected; no universal law/discovery claim is made before the withheld prediction succeeds.

## Current evidence against premature compression

Experiment 045A explicitly tested the conversational candidate `Core=(G,T,tau)` on the common frozen C1/C2/C3/C5 low-k structure block using

`R(z,k)=mu+T(k)+tau(z)+I(z,k)`.

The simple additive core **failed** the pre-frozen compact adequacy criteria. Designer f(R) carries about `29.99%` of its response power in irreducible scale-time interaction `I(k,z)` and the additive projection can distort pairwise geometry by up to `14.31 deg`; GDM carries about `4.4-4.5%` interaction power.

Therefore the universal-model project must **not** start from a hard-coded three-coordinate `G,T,tau` architecture. The new `I(k,z)` result is a candidate response signature to investigate, not evidence that exactly four parameters are sufficient.

C4 WDM also remains a domain-support warning: its informative response lives at high k, so family-complete dimensionality cannot be inferred from the common low-k matrix.

## Current working hypothesis

A compact basis may eventually exist, but current evidence favors a **multi-channel, possibly interaction-bearing response geometry** rather than a fixed list of independent scalar hairs. `docs/BUYANOVGPT_TABLE.md` is the live organizer for candidate response types; it is deliberately not a universal theory.

## Operational continuation

Continue the existing DSIR methodology:

- compare models and mechanisms first;
- seek new hard positive and negative results;
- preserve interesting pairwise and multi-channel reversals;
- map exact nulls and domain-localized effects;
- test scale-time interaction morphology and other cross-coordinate couplings;
- validate zeros instead of imputing them;
- extend the observationally meaningful response atlas;
- in parallel track `N_repr` and `N_disc` separately and stress-test their stability;
- when the readiness criteria above are met, use this document and the accumulated recovery/findings files as the launch point for the universal dark-sector model.
