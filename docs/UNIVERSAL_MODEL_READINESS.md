# DSIR universal-model readiness strategy

**Date:** 2026-08-25

## Strategic rule

DSIR must not prematurely collapse into a universal dark-sector model. The primary research program remains model-agnostic comparative reconstruction: embed qualitatively different dark-sector and modified-gravity families into common response channels, compare their geometry, identify nulls/degeneracies/sign reversals/channel migration, and record new hard or negative findings.

In parallel, estimate the minimum number of independent latent response coordinates/functions required by the validated atlas. This minimum dimension is a research output, not an assumption. A raw catalog SVD or a convenient three-channel summary is not by itself evidence for a three-parameter dark sector.

## Why this matters

The eventual universal model should be informed by the mature DSIR atlas rather than imposed on it. In particular, the future construction should be able to query the atlas for:

- which response blocks are genuinely independent after observational/gauge/precision validation;
- which families lie in exact or approximate null spaces of particular operators;
- which apparent degeneracies migrate or reverse between geometry, temporal growth, density/velocity, slip/lensing and small-scale transfer;
- how many latent coordinates are stable to family prior, within-family sampling, covariance, solver precision and channel removal;
- which observables fail as low-dimensional compressions for scale-dependent models;
- what withheld family/model can be reserved for a genuine out-of-sample prediction.

## Readiness criteria for beginning a universal model

Begin the dedicated universal-model construction only when all of the following are substantially satisfied:

1. **Comparative atlas maturity:** the main frozen families have validated mappings across the response blocks that are physically relevant for their mechanisms; missing blocks are distinguished from validated zeros.
2. **Cross-family semantic bridges:** gauge, total-matter/tracer and solver conventions are validated well enough that density, velocity, metric and transfer comparisons are meaningful.
3. **Observational layer maturity:** geometry, growth/RSD, shape/window and slip/lensing mappings are sufficiently validated that theory-space geometry is not being mistaken for survey distinguishability.
4. **Latent-dimension stability:** a candidate minimal number of independent coordinates/functions survives prior, sampling, covariance, precision and channel-removal stress tests. No fixed number, including three, is assumed beforehand.
5. **Withheld validation plan:** at least one physically distinct family/model or response regime remains hidden from model construction and can test genuine prediction rather than interpolation.
6. **Claim discipline:** G7/G8 boundaries remain respected; no universal law/discovery claim is made before the withheld prediction succeeds.

## Current working hypothesis

A compact basis may eventually organize itself into a small number of broad influence types such as geometry/background, dynamical clustering/time evolution, and metric/scale-dependent stress/transfer. However, current DSIR results already show that these broad labels can contain multiple distinct subchannels. Therefore the present task is to *measure* the minimal stable dimension, not to force it to three.

## Operational continuation

Continue the existing DSIR methodology:

- compare models and mechanisms first;
- seek new hard positive and negative results;
- preserve interesting pairwise and multi-channel reversals;
- validate zeros instead of imputing them;
- extend the observationally meaningful response atlas;
- in parallel track candidate minimal latent dimension and its stability;
- when the readiness criteria above are met, use this document and the accumulated recovery/findings files as the launch point for the universal dark-sector model.
