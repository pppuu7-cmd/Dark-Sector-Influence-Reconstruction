# DSIR response equivalence and manifold intersections

DSIR classifies model instances by observable response, not theory names. A model family maps to a manifold in response space; different family manifolds can overlap or coincide in restricted channels.

## Interacting vacuum <-> wCDM exact background map
With convention
`d rho_c/d ln a + 3 rho_c = +xi rho_de`, `d rho_de/d ln a = -xi rho_de`, and vacuum-like `w_de=-1`, the total flat late-time background is
`E^2(a)=A a^-3 + (1-A) a^-xi`, which is exactly the constant-w CDM form under `Omega_m,eff=A` and `w_eff=-1+xi/3`. No background expansion observable can distinguish the descriptions after this mapping.

## Exact/controlled intersections
- interacting vacuum intersects LambdaCDM at `xi=0`;
- generalized Chaplygin gas intersects exact Lambda+matter at `alpha=0`;
- WDM+Lambda shares the homogeneous pressureless-matter background in the control approximation but differs in scale-dependent structure;
- designer f(R) can reproduce LambdaCDM background while differing in growth/slip/screening.

A low-dimensional background manifold is therefore not evidence for one microscopic origin. DSIR records equivalence maps and the additional discriminant channels that break them.
