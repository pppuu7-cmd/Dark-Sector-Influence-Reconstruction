# DSIR article-series roadmap v0.1

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Status:** manuscript planning; does not alter scientific gates

The publication plan is deliberately staged so that methodological results can be published without waiting for a discovery claim, while stronger observational/new-physics claims remain blocked by G7/G8/G9.

## Article DSIR-1 — observable-response geometry

**Provisional title**  
*Dark-Sector Influence Reconstruction I: Observable-response geometry, channel-conditional equivalence and failure-resistant model comparison*

**Primary question**  
What reproducible structure survives when heterogeneous dark-sector, dark-energy, interacting and modified-gravity models are compared in a common response language while preserving solver provenance, missing domains and negative results?

**Core material**

- residual-source / response-space architecture;
- same-solver reference quotients and cross-solver validation logic;
- block-aware multi-family atlas;
- exact-null patterns as part of model identity;
- scale/time localization and irreducible `k×z` interaction structure;
- finite-amplitude trajectory curvature;
- distinction `N_micro`, `N_manifold`, `N_repr`, `N_disc`;
- characteristic-scale/window-crossing tests;
- cross-model translator as a channel-conditional rather than global parameter map;
- known-sector F30 controls showing that matter-only geometry is not dark-specific;
- preserved hard failures/nulls as part of the result;
- narrow prior-art/novelty boundary.

**Claims explicitly excluded**

- no discovery of new dark-sector physics;
- no universal residual law;
- no claim that model manifolds/PCA/information geometry are new;
- no direct C5 Weyl-provider claim while C5 certification is incomplete;
- no G7/G8/G9 promotion.

**Dependency:** does not require G7/G8/G9. It must rely only on already validated/descriptive theory-response results and clearly label prospective versus retrospective evidence.

## Article DSIR-2 — independent matter/Weyl physical discriminants

**Provisional title**  
*Dark-Sector Influence Reconstruction II: Solver-validated matter–Weyl response bridges and mechanism discriminants*

**Primary question**  
How much model ambiguity is removed when matter growth and Weyl/lensing response are treated as independent physical channels rather than inferred from one another?

**Required before drafting as a result paper**

- certified C3 physical provider;
- separately certified C5 physical provider under the Exp069G minimum contract;
- common support-validity mask frozen only after provider certification;
- signed `P_Wm`, `P_mm`, `P_WW` semantics locked;
- at least one known-sector control in the same joint-channel representation;
- explicit treatment of C4 high-k-only support rather than zero imputation.

This paper may remain theory/solver-facing; it does not itself require an observational dark-sector detection.

## Article DSIR-3 — observation-space quotient reconstruction

**Provisional title**  
*Dark-Sector Influence Reconstruction III: Covariance-whitened, nuisance-quotiented multi-probe reconstruction*

**Primary question**  
What dark-sector response directions remain identifiable after released survey covariance, window operators and nuisance tangents are applied?

**Required**

- physical providers and common support mask;
- ACT DR6 × unWISE selected covariance/window bridge;
- covariance restriction/whitening;
- preregistered nuisance SVD/rank rule;
- quotient-space reconstruction;
- DESI/ACT/unWISE channel bookkeeping as applicable;
- completed G7 relation/null test.

A null G7 result is still publishable if the observational identifiability/quotient analysis is complete and scientifically informative.

## Article DSIR-4 — genuinely withheld-family falsification

**Provisional title**  
*Dark-Sector Influence Reconstruction IV: Prospective withheld-family tests of cross-channel regularities*

**Primary question**  
Does a relation frozen on eligible training families plus known-sector controls predict a genuinely unseen physical family?

**Required**

- a frozen G7 candidate or frozen G7 null framework;
- fresh family withheld from discovery/training;
- prospective G8 protocol;
- no parameter/family replacement after inspection;
- G8 classification and, if applicable, G9 interpretation.

Only this stage can support strong language about a candidate cross-family dark-sector regularity. Even a G8 PASS is not automatically evidence for a new fundamental law.

## Article RTK–DSIR-S — later comparative synthesis

**Provisional title**  
*Inverse physical inference across distinct theory programs: methodological lessons from RTK and DSIR*

**Purpose**

Compare the independently developed RTK and DSIR programs at the level of inverse-problem architecture, identifiability, effective coordinates, solver validation, negative results and withheld-test logic.

**Hard boundary**

- RTK and DSIR remain separate repositories and evidence chains;
- each side must already support at least one independent manuscript before this comparative synthesis is considered mature;
- only immutable cross-references are imported;
- no physical equivalence is inferred from formal similarity alone.

The detailed boundary is frozen in `RTK_DSIR_PUBLICATION_BOUNDARY_V0_1.md`.

## Dependency graph

`DSIR-1` can proceed independently from the current theory-response record.

`validated C3 + certified C5 -> DSIR-2 -> common support/covariance/nuisance quotient -> DSIR-3/G7 -> fresh G8 -> DSIR-4`

RTK publication work proceeds independently in RTK. A later `RTK–DSIR-S` synthesis depends on mature outputs from both programs but does not merge their scientific gates.

## Editorial rule

Do not delay a narrowly supportable methodological paper merely because a later discovery gate is open. Conversely, do not import the rhetorical strength of a later planned paper into an earlier manuscript. Each article must carry its own claim boundary and evidence matrix.
