## 2. High-risk prior art found

### 2.1 Bashinsky (2007), “Mapping Cosmological Observables to the Dark Kinetics”

arXiv:0707.0692.

Established prior content includes:

- systematic mapping from inhomogeneous dark-sector properties to CMB/LSS signatures;
- sound speed/stiffness and anisotropic stress as dark-kinetics coordinates;
- different observables probe different gravitational potentials/epochs;
- propagation speed and clustering map to distinctive observable features;
- a warning that nonstandard growth or `Phi/Psi` is not by itself a definitive modified-gravity signature.

**DSIR consequence:** the general arrow

`dark microphysics -> observable feature`

is prior art, as is the caution that response features are not unique theory identifiers.

### 2.2 Sapone & Kunz (2009), “Fingerprinting dark energy”

Phys. Rev. D 80, 083519; DOI `10.1103/PhysRevD.80.083519`.

The paper explicitly uses **fingerprint** language for perturbation properties that distinguish dark-energy models sharing similar background behavior, and derives how equation of state and sound speed affect matter/CMB observables.

**DSIR consequence:** neither the word “fingerprint” nor the basic perturbation-fingerprint idea is novel and should not be used as a priority claim.
