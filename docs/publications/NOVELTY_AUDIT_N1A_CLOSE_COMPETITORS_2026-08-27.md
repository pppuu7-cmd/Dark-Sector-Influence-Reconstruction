## Purpose

N0 established that many DSIR ingredients have substantial prior art. N1A narrows the manuscript claim further by checking close methodological competitors that overlap the current DSIR-1 scope: dark degeneracy/effective sources, theory dictionaries, representative-theory PCA, nuisance projection, simulator misspecification and organized dark-sector theory spaces.

This audit is intentionally conservative. Failure to find an exact duplicate does not prove that none exists.

## Close competitors and consequences

### 1. Kunz — dark degeneracy

Martin Kunz, *The dark degeneracy: On the number and nature of dark components*, arXiv:astro-ph/0702615; later Phys. Rev. D 80, 123001.

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0702615

Established overlap:

- gravity constrains the total stress-energy rather than a unique DM/DE decomposition;
- interacting and noninteracting descriptions can be gravitationally degenerate;
- observable-oriented parametrization is preferable to assuming a unique microscopic split.

**DSIR consequence:** neither the dark degeneracy nor residual-source philosophy is novel.

### 2. Hu & Sawicki — PPF and effective dark-energy correspondence

Wayne Hu, Ignacy Sawicki, *A Parameterized Post-Friedmann Framework for Modified Gravity*, Phys. Rev. D 76, 104043 (2007), arXiv:0708.1190.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.76.104043

Established overlap:

- modified gravity can be represented by an effective stress tensor;
- MG and generalized dark energy are formally equivalent at that level;
- distinguishing physics enters through closure relations and scale-regime behavior.

**DSIR consequence:** `X_mu_nu` is an organizing definition, not an invention.

### 3. Baker, Ferreira & Skordis — PPF theory dictionary

T. Baker, P. G. Ferreira, C. Skordis, *The parameterized post-Friedmann framework for theories of modified gravity: Concepts, formalism, and examples*, Phys. Rev. D 87, 024015 (2013), arXiv:1209.2117.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.87.024015

Established overlap:

- unified perturbation parametrization across broad MG models;
- explicit correspondence/dictionary between underlying theories and common variables.

**DSIR consequence:** a common theory dictionary is prior art. DSIR-1 must distinguish its heterogeneous DM+DE+interaction+MG response comparison and its validation/falsification architecture rather than claim the dictionary itself.

### 4. Alsing & Wandelt — nuisance-hardened cosmological compression

Justin Alsing, Benjamin Wandelt, *Nuisance hardened data compression for fast likelihood-free inference*, MNRAS 488, 5093–5103 (2019), arXiv:1903.01473.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz1900

Established overlap:

- nuisance sensitivity can be projected from cosmological compressed summaries;
- Fisher/score geometry supplies an explicit nuisance-hardening operator;
- demonstrated on supernova and weak-lensing inference.

**DSIR consequence:** nuisance projection/hardening is not a new concept. The planned DSIR quotient is distinguished only by where it sits in the provenance-controlled heterogeneous-theory pipeline and by its explicit physical-provider/support-mask prerequisites.

### 5. Zanoletti & Leonard — PCA from representative modified-gravity theories

C. M. A. Zanoletti, C. D. Leonard, *Principal components for model-agnostic modified gravity with 3x2pt data*, Phys. Rev. D 112, 063547 (2025), arXiv:2503.20951.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/ng53-k782

Established overlap:

- PCA features constructed from a bank of representative gravity theories;
- transformed data space used to retain nonlinear information while mitigating model dependence;
- 3x2pt growth/lensing applications and degeneracy breaking.

**Threat level:** HIGH for any broad claim that DSIR is the first to compress a representative theory bank or combine matter/lensing information.

**DSIR consequence:** DSIR-1 must not claim novelty from PCA/SVD over model spectra. Its distinct evidence is instead the block-aware heterogeneous mechanism atlas, explicit missing-domain semantics, channel-conditional equivalence, curved/backtracking trajectory diagnostics, and prospective failure/withheld ledger.

### 6. Pierre et al. — cosmological simulator misspecification/OOD robustness

S. Pierre, B. Régaldo-Saint Blancard, C. Hahn, M. Eickenberg, *Mitigating model misspecification in simulation-based inference for galaxy clustering*, Phys. Rev. D 113, 043536 (2026), arXiv:2507.03086.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/gypc-sqnx

Established overlap:

- cosmological inference can fail when observations are outside the simulator/training support;
- inconsistent summary components can be detected/discarded;
- robust inference requires explicit model-misspecification handling.

Related 2025 field-level/OOD work also demonstrates catastrophic cross-simulator failure under resolution/model differences.

**DSIR consequence:** out-of-distribution or withheld robustness testing is not itself novel. DSIR should claim only its specific **physical-family prospective withholding** and failure-preservation logic if used.

### 7. Naeem — LambdaCDM as fixed point / controlled dark-sector deformations

Marriam Naeem, *LambdaCDM as a fixed point: Controlled dark-sector deformations and late-time structure growth*, Annals of Physics 490, 170466 (2026).

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aop.2026.170466

Established overlap:

- LambdaCDM treated as a fixed point in a larger dark-sector theory space;
- controlled perturbative deformations organized around that point;
- scale/redshift growth and weak-lensing signatures emphasized;
- model-independent classification language used.

**Threat level:** HIGH for claims such as “first organized dark-sector theory space”, “first residual dark-sector deformation atlas”, or “first model-independent classification”.

**DSIR consequence:** the manuscript must emphasize that DSIR ingests heterogeneous pre-existing physical model families and studies their response equivalence/falsification under explicit solver/channel provenance rather than presenting fixed-point theory-space organization as new.

### 8. Historical weak-lensing/growth degeneracy-breaking literature

Modified-gravity weak-lensing analyses have long emphasized that expansion, growth and metric-potential/lensing information can break degeneracies that background data alone cannot.

Representative example:

- *Constraining modified gravity and growth with weak lensing*, MNRAS 395, 197 (2009).

**DSIR consequence:** “add lensing to growth to break degeneracy” is not novel. The potentially distinctive object is the systematic **channel-conditional equivalence-class construction across heterogeneous families**, together with solver validation, missing-domain masking and later real covariance/nuisance quotient.

## What DSIR-1 must NOT claim

The manuscript must not state or imply that DSIR introduced:

1. dark degeneracy;
2. effective dark-sector stress tensors/residual sources;
3. PPF/EFT-style common theory dictionaries;
4. model manifolds or information geometry;
5. PCA/SVD of representative cosmological/MG theory banks;
6. nuisance projection/hardening;
7. adding lensing to growth to break MG/DE degeneracy;
8. out-of-distribution/model-misspecification validation;
9. prospective/withheld validation as a generic scientific idea;
10. LambdaCDM as a fixed point or an organized dark-sector theory space.
