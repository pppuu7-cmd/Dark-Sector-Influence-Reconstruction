## Observation-space support closure and perturbativity

A survey-level quotient is meaningful only after the physical support of the realized observational operator has been shown to lie inside a certified theory domain. This requirement is logically prior to covariance whitening and nuisance projection: statistical weighting cannot repair an undefined or physically unjustified theory response.

DSIR tested this ordering explicitly. With the preregistered 5% leakage threshold, the current certified C3/C5 domain retains **0 of 26** ACTxunWISE candidate coordinates. A prospective joint lower-redshift/upper-wavenumber planning search can recover 15 coordinates only by extending the nominal domain to approximately

\[
z_{\min}=0.0087346,\qquad
k_{\max}=4.81826\ {\rm Mpc}^{-1},
\]

corresponding descriptively to an upper-\(k\) extension by a factor 72.29. This is geometry, not a provider certification. Under the separately frozen GR-reference linear perturbativity audit, only 7 of 64 source pairs satisfy the primary \(\Delta^2\le1\) condition and the retained observational dimension remains zero. The tested linear route is therefore physically ineligible on the planning frontier.

Prospective follow-up audits show why this failure cannot be repaired by a hidden numerical substitution. The projection architecture itself can accept independent matter, signed Weyl--matter and Weyl-auto blocks, but the existing certified/public stack does not provide a justified nonlinear three-block realization over the required domain. For designer \(f(R)\), the missing nonlinear layer is principally a provider/calibration problem because the underlying nonlinear theory is defined in principle. For the frozen phenomenological GDM pressure/shear family, by contrast, nonlinear continuation is not uniquely fixed by the linear closure; choosing one completion would add model-defining assumptions. Paper I therefore does not treat a retrospectively selected nonlinear GDM continuation as the unchanged C3 model.

A second independent prerequisite is that the positive support measure itself be normalizable. In the frozen KiDS absolute-response test, all eight Wm and all eight WW components remain nonnormalizable as the harmonic cutoff is extended. The measured local exponents stay near \(p\simeq1.5\), with order-unity dyadic-shell contributions rather than convergence to a finite denominator. DSIR therefore does not introduce a retrospective ultraviolet cutoff or fiducial-power weighting merely to manufacture a finite support fraction.

A finite operator class is still not enough: its **exact real-data realization** must be reproducible before a physical-support statistic is evaluated. One harmonic DES candidate passed the finite-operator-class screen but failed this exact-realization provenance gate. A prospectively selected public DES Y1 replacement then passed successive source-identity, mask/redshift-distribution and raw-row-to-HEALPix reproduction prerequisites under unchanged future support criteria. These are reproduction prerequisites only; no physical-support PASS, covariance distance or nuisance-quotiented survey separation is inferred from them. The detailed experiment-by-experiment chronology and exact machine classifications are moved to the supplementary observation-route ledger and canonical provenance matrix.

The support mask must act consistently on both the projection and covariance before whitening,

\[
K_{B,{\rm supp}}=M_{\rm supp}K_B,
\qquad
C_{B,{\rm supp}}=M_{\rm supp}C_BM_{\rm supp}^{T},
\]

followed only then by

\[
W_{B,{\rm supp}}=C_{B,{\rm supp}}^{-1/2},
\qquad
A_{B,{\rm supp}}=Q_{B,{\rm supp}}W_{B,{\rm supp}}K_{B,{\rm supp}}.
\]

Consequently DSIR does **not** compute or quote a covariance-whitened, nuisance-quotiented survey distance from a route that fails finite normalization, exact reproducibility, physical-domain support, or provider/model semantics.
