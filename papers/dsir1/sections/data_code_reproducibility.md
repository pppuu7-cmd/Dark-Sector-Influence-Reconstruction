# Data, code, and reproducibility

DSIR-I is designed so that the scientific record does not depend on conversational context or on a final plotting notebook. The analysis code, frozen experiment summaries, validation contracts, scientific PASS/FAIL classifications, and manuscript claim ledger are maintained in the public `Dark-Sector-Influence-Reconstruction` repository. Each central quantitative manuscript statement is linked in `papers/dsir1/PROVENANCE_MATRIX.md` or a manuscript-scoped evidence/provenance extension to a workflow run, immutable GitHub Actions artifact or commit, SHA256 digest where available, frozen criterion, and interpretation boundary.

The paper distinguishes four forms of reproducibility evidence. First, **operator controls** verify identities internal to the analysis, including reconstruction closure, orthogonality, normalization, signed-spectrum semantics, and repeatability. Second, **numerical-provider controls** verify solver limits, source state, unit conventions, interpolation semantics, and state integrity. Third, **scientific prospective controls** freeze a prediction or acceptance rule before evaluating a new amplitude, family, or mechanism whenever the result is intended to support a directional claim rather than provide descriptive morphology. Fourth, **public-input reproduction controls** bind exact released survey objects by checksum and reproduce masks, redshift distributions, or operator inputs before any physical-support or downstream likelihood quantity is read.

Scientific failures are immutable provenance. A failed experiment is not reclassified when a later experiment identifies a justified correction. For example, the original C3 GDM target-grid physical-power bridge remains failed at a roughly 4.75% reconstruction defect; a later mechanism audit localized the defect to the frozen interpolation construction, and a separately preregistered native-grid provider passed at a \(2.81\times10^{-14}\) matter-power closure. Likewise, the original designer-\(f(R)\) q=1 bridge remains failed against the frozen \(5\times10^{-6}\) GR-limit criterion even though a prospective numerical-accuracy ladder and separately frozen q=3 provider subsequently pass the same physical boundary without floor subtraction or threshold relaxation.

The same rule applies to physical generalization. A common C3/C5 full-response-centroid relation was frozen before evaluation on a withheld IDM--DR mechanism and failed with all four slopes of the opposite sign to the calibration. That failure remains part of the paper evidence. The withheld family is not reused as a fresh test for any new relation whose form was selected after inspecting that failure.

Undefined theory/channel cells are never zero-imputed. Cross-family statistics are evaluated only on explicitly defined support, and high-\(k\) WDM products remain a distinct block from the frozen low-\(k\) C1/C2/C3/C5 atlas. Theory-space response angles, \(\chi_I\), and \(\eta_I\) are not reported as signal-to-noise ratios or survey-level significances. The final observational signature construction additionally requires a support-eligible physical/window operator, validated covariance whitening, and a prospectively frozen nuisance quotient. A support fraction is itself treated as undefined when the prospectively chosen positive operator measure is nonnormalizable; no retrospective ultraviolet cutoff or fiducial-power weighting is introduced merely to force a finite normalization.

The late DES harmonic-route work illustrates the public-input boundary explicitly. Exp073P2 completed SHA256 identity binding for every DES Y1 release object prospectively frozen by that route, including two multi-gigabyte catalogues, before support scoring. Exp073S0 then reproduced the native \(N_{\rm side}=4096\) redMaGiC mask and the public lens/source redshift-distribution tables under immutable workflow/artifact provenance. Those PASSes close input-identity and small-input reproduction prerequisites only: they do not by themselves establish a physical support mask, read covariance values, determine a nuisance rank, or advance G7/G8/G9.

Publication figures are generated from repository products under `papers/dsir1/figures/`. The figure manifest records the exact input products, scientific selection rules, and caption boundaries. Plotting scripts may change presentation details, but they may not silently change the scientific domain, mask, normalization, response orientation, or frozen threshold. Final figure outputs are accompanied by checksums in the build artifact.
