# Data, code, and reproducibility

DSIR-I is designed so that the scientific record does not depend on conversational context or on a final plotting notebook. The analysis code, frozen experiment summaries, validation contracts, scientific PASS/FAIL classifications, and manuscript claim ledger are maintained in the public `Dark-Sector-Influence-Reconstruction` repository. Each central quantitative manuscript statement is linked in `papers/dsir1/PROVENANCE_MATRIX.md` or a manuscript-scoped evidence/provenance extension to a workflow run, immutable GitHub Actions artifact or commit, SHA256 digest where available, frozen criterion, and interpretation boundary.

The paper distinguishes four forms of reproducibility evidence. First, **operator controls** verify identities internal to the analysis, including reconstruction closure, orthogonality, normalization, signed-spectrum semantics, and repeatability. Second, **numerical-provider controls** verify solver limits, source state, unit conventions, interpolation semantics, and state integrity. Third, **scientific prospective controls** freeze a prediction or acceptance rule before evaluating a new amplitude, family, mechanism, or operator realization whenever the result is intended to support a directional claim rather than provide descriptive morphology. Fourth, **public-input reproduction controls** bind exact released survey objects by checksum and reproduce masks, redshift distributions, catalog-to-pixel mappings, or other operator inputs before any physical-support or downstream likelihood quantity is read.

Scientific failures are immutable provenance. A failed experiment is not reclassified when a later experiment identifies a justified correction. For example, the original C3 GDM target-grid physical-power bridge remains failed at a roughly 4.75% reconstruction defect; a later mechanism audit localized the defect to the frozen interpolation construction, and a separately preregistered native-grid provider passed at a \(2.81\times10^{-14}\) matter-power closure. Likewise, the original designer-\(f(R)\) q=1 bridge remains failed against the frozen \(5\times10^{-6}\) GR-limit criterion even though a prospective numerical-accuracy ladder and separately frozen q=3 provider subsequently pass the same physical boundary without floor subtraction or threshold relaxation.

The same rule applies to physical generalization and observational-operator construction. A common C3/C5 full-response-centroid relation was frozen before evaluation on a withheld IDM--DR mechanism and failed with all four slopes of the opposite sign to the calibration. That failure remains part of the paper evidence. Likewise, Exp073M identified a finite-positive DES harmonic operator class, but the independently frozen Exp073N exact-realization audit failed mandatory reproduction/provenance before any support fraction was computed. DSIR therefore did not relabel the candidate as usable; Exp073N remains failed while Exp073O prospectively selected a public Cosmotheka DES Y1 real-data replacement under unchanged future support criteria.

Undefined theory/channel cells are never zero-imputed. Cross-family statistics are evaluated only on explicitly defined support, and high-\(k\) WDM products remain a distinct block from the frozen low-\(k\) C1/C2/C3/C5 atlas. Theory-space response angles, \(\chi_I\), and \(\eta_I\) are not reported as signal-to-noise ratios or survey-level significances. The final observational signature construction additionally requires a finite positive support measure, an exact reproducible real-data operator/input realization, a support-eligible physical/window operator, validated covariance whitening, and a prospectively frozen nuisance quotient. A support fraction is itself treated as undefined when the prospectively chosen positive operator measure is nonnormalizable; no retrospective ultraviolet cutoff or fiducial-power weighting is introduced merely to force a finite normalization.

The late DES harmonic-route work illustrates this hierarchy explicitly. Exp073O replaced the provenance-incomplete Y3 Wm realization with a public finite DES Y1 redMaGiC x Metacal pseudo-\(C_\ell\) operator while leaving the future 5% physical-support threshold and minimum retained dimension unchanged. Exp073P2 then completed SHA256 identity binding for every frozen DES Y1 release object, including two multi-gigabyte catalogues, before support scoring. Exp073S0 reproduced the native \(N_{\rm side}=4096\) redMaGiC mask and the public lens/source redshift-distribution tables under immutable workflow/artifact provenance.

Exp073R0 subsequently passed the preregistered raw-row/HEALPix equivalence prerequisite after two earlier infrastructure-incomplete transport attempts were preserved. It sampled 131,072 rows from the 136,930,995-row parent catalogue across 16 frozen windows and recovered exact required source/metacalibration fields and exact HEALPix pixel indices in all four source bins at \(N_{\rm side}=4096\), `coords=C`. The R0 artifact explicitly records `science_gate_scored=false`; consequently this PASS closes only an operator-reproduction prerequisite. It does not establish a physical support mask, read covariance values, determine a nuisance rank, or advance G7/G8/G9. Exp073R1 is preregistered and gated but is not treated as a result until a completed output exists.

Publication figures are generated from repository products under `papers/dsir1/figures/`. The figure manifest records the exact input products, scientific selection rules, and caption boundaries. Plotting scripts may change presentation details, but they may not silently change the scientific domain, mask, normalization, response orientation, or frozen threshold. Final figure outputs are accompanied by checksums in the build artifact.

The manuscript itself is maintained on the dedicated branch `paper/dsir-i-observable-response-geometry` and reviewed through draft pull request #99. Chat-independent recovery checkpoints are stored under `recovery/`; recovery should proceed from repository files rather than reconstructed conversational memory.
