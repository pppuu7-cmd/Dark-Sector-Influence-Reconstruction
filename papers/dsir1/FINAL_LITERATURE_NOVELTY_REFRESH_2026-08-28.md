## Closest neighbors confirmed in the refresh

**Boundary:** strong direct precedent for channel-dependent degeneracy breaking in a specific pair; not a heterogeneous response atlas or operator-kernel quotient framework.

### 2. Current DESI-era interacting-dark-sector analyses
